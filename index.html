05 / QA WORKFLOW

From requirement to release.

A repeatable testing process helps uncover risk early and keeps releases dependable.

01

Understand requirements

Review business requirements and user stories; clarify acceptance criteria and identify high-risk areas.

02

Design test coverage

Prepare test scenarios, detailed test cases, test data, and traceability to requirements.

03

Execute and report

Run planned and exploratory tests, log clear defects in JIRA, and work with developers to reproduce issues.

04

Retest and sign off

Verify fixes, perform regression and post-release smoke tests, then summarize results for release decisions.
