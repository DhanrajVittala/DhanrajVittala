05 / DEBUGGING PHILOSOPHY

My process.

Structured troubleshooting is a superpower. Here's the loop.

01

Observe the signal

Understand symptoms and gather useful evidence before changing anything.

02

Isolate the cause

Reproduce the issue and narrow down what changed or failed.

03

Apply a safe fix

Make a targeted change and keep the impact in mind.

04

Verify and document

Confirm service is restored and capture the resolution for next time.
